    Day 1 · Your room

    IT STARTS IN YOUR ROOM.

    You pick up the guitar. The chord buzzes. The tabs don't sound like the song, and the video is too fast to follow. After a while the guitar goes back on its stand.

    Sound familiar? You're not bad at guitar. You've just been practising alone.

    Day 1 · Later that night

    THEN YOU OPEN SMART GUITAR.

    You pick a song you love. The guitar part is pulled out of the real recording, each chord shows up right when it's played, and the beats under it tell you how long to hold it.

    For the first time, it sounds like the song.
