Brand Guidelines

SMART GUITAR

The visual identity system for Smart Guitar — an AI-powered music practice tool that transforms any song into an interactive learning experience.

v1.0 — April 2026
01 / Overview
BRAND ESSENCE
Smart Guitar uses AI to split any song into individual instrument stems, detect chords in real time, sync lyrics, and generate guitar tabs. Musicians play along as if they're in the band.
Brand Personality

Bold — unapologetic, direct, confident
Fiery — passionate, energetic, alive
Empowering — removes barriers to playing
Modern — cutting-edge AI, sleek dark UI
Accessible — free to try, no credit card

Voice & Tone

Short, punchy, imperative sentences.
Action-oriented: “Start Playing Free.”
Second person: “You decide.”
No jargon — speaks to musicians, not engineers.
Never passive or apologetic.

Tagline
Your smart guitar companion

Domain: smart-guitar.com
App: app.smart-guitar.com

02 / Logo
LOGO SYSTEM
The Smart Guitar logo features an electric guitar engulfed in flames — a dramatic, photorealistic illustration on pure black. Two variants serve different contexts.
Primary — App Icon
App Icon Variant
Detailed electric guitar with realistic fire. Used for app icon, favicon, PWA icon, and in-app branding.
Secondary — Marketing
Marketing Variant
Guitar silhouette fully consumed by a ring of fire. More dramatic and abstract. Used in hero sections, ads, and splash screens.

LOGO USAGE RULES

  • Always place on pure black (#0a0a0a) or very dark backgrounds
  • Never place on light or white backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space equal to the logo's own width on all sides
  • Never add drop shadows, outlines, or additional effects
  • Never rotate, stretch, crop, or recolor the flames
  • Never overlay text directly on the logo

04 / Typography
THREE VOICES
A three-font system: Bebas Neue commands attention, Inter communicates clearly, JetBrains Mono handles musical precision.
Bebas Neue Display • Regular 400 • Always uppercase
SEE THE CHORDS.
FEEL THE RHYTHM.

Role: Hero headlines, section titles, bold statements. Bebas Neue is the brand's loudest voice — cinematic, condensed, and always in full caps. Never use for body text or small UI labels.
Inter Sans-serif • 400–900 • Primary body font
Regular 400 — AI splits any song into vocals, guitar, bass, drums, and piano. Medium 500 — See every chord. Follow the strumming pattern. Semibold 600 — Play like you're in the band. Bold 700 — Search for Any Song Extrabold 800 — Start Playing Free
Role: All readable content — body text, button labels, descriptions, navigation items, UI controls. Inter is optimized for screen readability at all sizes.

Role: Chord labels, guitar tab notation, section labels, technical metadata. JetBrains Mono provides the precision needed for musical data where character alignment matters.

05 / Imagery
VISUAL LANGUAGE
Dark, dramatic, fire-lit. Every image reinforces the brand's energy and warmth against deep black backgrounds.
Hero Banner — Fire Strings
Social — 9:16 Story
Detail — Fretboard Close-Up
Brand Texture

Subtle ember particle texture for dark backgrounds and ad compositions

DO
  • Use dark, dramatic backgrounds with warm fire/amber lighting
  • Show real musicians in natural practice environments
  • Apply rounded corners (12–16px) on photo containers
  • Use dark overlays/vignettes to blend images into the UI
  • Keep the fire as the primary/only light source
  • Use portrait framing for testimonial photos
DON'T
  • Use bright, high-key or white-background photography
  • Show generic stock photos of hands on keyboards
  • Add borders, frames, or drop shadows around photos
  • Use cool-toned (blue/green) lighting or filters
  • Place images without dark overlay on light surfaces
  • Mix illustration styles with the photorealistic fire motif
06 / UI Components
DESIGN SYSTEM
Core interactive elements that make up the Smart Guitar interface. All components are built for dark backgrounds only.

BUTTONS

Primary CTA
Golden Action
Secondary
Ghost

Primary: fire-500 bg, white text. Golden: flame-400 bg, dark text. Secondary: transparent, white border. Ghost: transparent, charcoal border. All use 8px radius, 600 weight, Inter font.

CARDS

Standard Card

Charcoal-800 background, charcoal-600 border, 12px radius. No drop shadows.

Accent Card

Same as standard with a 3px fire-600 left border for emphasis and visual hierarchy.

Elevated Card

Charcoal-900 background for a sunken/elevated feel. Elevation via background lightness, not shadow.

INPUTS

Semi-transparent charcoal-800 background. Charcoal-600 border, flame-400 on focus. Smoke-500 placeholder text. Matching button radius.

07 / Motion
ANIMATION
Motion is subtle and purposeful. Three signature animations reinforce the fire metaphor while keeping the interface fast and responsive.
Flame Pulse

Gentle golden glow pulsing via box-shadow. 2s infinite loop. Used on the active play button.

Favorite Ignite

Scale to 1.3x with brightness boost, 0.3s ease-out. Hover to preview. Used when favoriting a song.

Slide Up
Vocals Guitar Bass

Opacity 0→1 with 1rem upward translation, 0.3s ease-out. Used for content entrances.

Motion Principles
Animate only transform and opacity
Duration: 200–300ms for UI, 2s for ambient loops
Always honor prefers-reduced-motion
Easing: ease-out entrances, ease-in-out loops

10 / Brand Don'ts
GUARD RAILS
Rules to keep the brand consistent and recognizable across all touchpoints.
×

Never use light or white backgrounds for primary compositions

×

Never set Bebas Neue in lowercase or mixed case — always full uppercase

×

Never use colors outside the palette (no blues, greens, or purples)

×

Never use thin or light font weights for headlines

×

Never show the product without the dark theme

×

Never use generic stock music photography

×

Never add gradients outside the fire/flame color family

×

Never compete fire-500 and flame-400 at the same hierarchy level — one leads, one supports

×

Never use transition: all — list specific properties
